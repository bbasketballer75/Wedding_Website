## Layout & Structure

- **Queue panel**: Anchored to FAB in bottom-right, expands upward with slide-up animation
- **Panel width**: 320px max, full-width on mobile (<400px viewport)
- **Panel height**: Auto (max 60vh with scroll)
- **FAB position**: Fixed, 24px from bottom, 24px from right (respects mobile safe areas)
- **Z-index**: FAB at z-50, Queue panel at z-50 (above gallery content)
- **Selection overlay**: Absolute positioned checkbox badge on photo corner
