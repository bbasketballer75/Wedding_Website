## Execution log (2026-09-12)

### Lessons learned (worth saving)

1. **Parallel agents writing to the same checkout is dangerous.** When I dispatched the Plan A subagent on `refactor/gallery-split-2026-09-12` and simultaneously ran `npm install` on `chore/jsdom-30`, the working directory was shared. My `git stash -u` while the subagent was mid-write **deleted its untracked files** (`data.ts`, `useGalleryData.ts`, etc.). The subagent's 600s timeout was spent trying to recover files that no longer existed on disk. **Rule:** one agent, one branch, one cwd at a time.

2. **PR #76 (security audit fix) already covered 9 of 11 open Dependabot PRs.** Closing them with explanatory comments was the right move rather than letting them auto-close at 30 days.

3. **jsdom 30 breaks vitest 4.1 on Linux CI** with `webidl.util.markAsUncloneable is not a function`. jsdom 29.1.1 (last pre-removal version) is the latest compatible major. Vitest 5 fixes this but requires Node 22+; project is on Node 20.19.

4. **ESLint 10 ecosystem is not ready.** `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y@6.10.2` and `eslint-plugin-react@7.37.x` cap at ESLint 9. Plan C must wait.

5. **TypeScript 6 + typescript-eslint v8 works on TS 6 API but not TS 7.** typescript-eslint has explicit guidance: until v8.45+ (or v9), run with TS 6 API. This blocks Plan B.2 until typescript-eslint ships TS 7 support.
